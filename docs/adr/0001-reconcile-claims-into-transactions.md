# Reconcile claims into transactions

Billzebub receives overlapping and sometimes imperfect reports of the same financial movement from real-time alerts, Apple Pay, and monthly statements. It will preserve each extracted report as an immutable Claim; a validated Statement Claim establishes a trusted Transaction either alone or by confirming a Real-time Claim, with the Real-time Claim supplying occurrence time and Authorized Amount and the Statement Claim supplying Posted Amount and posting details. Reconciliation uses an auditable deterministic evidence score: Financial Account must match, exact monetary evidence is preferred, cross-currency amounts may use an initial ±10% reference-rate band, and only a strong candidate clearly better than its alternatives is confirmed automatically; ambiguous pairings create Pending Review, while a Real-time Claim remains Provisional until statement history extends at least 14 days beyond occurrence and may later move from Unconfirmed to Confirmed when late evidence arrives.
